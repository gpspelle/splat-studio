Steps

The selection tool isn't installed yet

It uses SAM 2, an open model from Meta that runs on your Mac. About 1 GB, installed once.

1Frames

The video is cut into frames, as for a run. They're reused when you start it.

2Click

Choose Keep and click the object. Choose Leave out and click a stand, hand or holder. A few clicks on one frame are usually enough; add some on another frame where it goes wrong.

3Follow through the video

Applies your clicks to every frame. Scrub through afterwards and fix any frame where it slipped.

4Back to the run

Your selection is saved with the video. Set the quality and the other options on New run, then start it there.
