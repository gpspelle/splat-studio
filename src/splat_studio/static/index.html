    How to record a video that works the first time

    The software can only rebuild what the camera saw clearly, from many angles, in one continuous take.

    Avoid
    • Glass, mirrors, chrome, very shiny paint
    • Plain white tables and blank walls
    • Turntables (the background must not move)
    • People, pets or curtains moving in view
    • Cuts, pauses with fast pans, zooming

    1Pick the spot

    • Room to walk all the way around the object, at a steady 1-2 m distance.
    • Put it on a textured surface - a rug, newspaper, patterned cloth. COLMAP tracks texture, not plain surfaces.
    • Matte objects work best. Cover or skip reflective and transparent parts.

    2Get the light right

    • Soft, even light: overcast daylight or a bright room with several lamps.
    • No harsh sun or a single spotlight - hard shadows and highlights move as you walk.
    • Don't change the lights during the take, and keep your own shadow off the object.

    3Set up the phone

    • 4K at 30 fps, landscape. Stay on the 1× lens and never zoom (the phone would switch cameras).
    • Lock focus and exposure: press and hold on the object until "AE/AF Lock" appears.
    • Turn off Cinematic and Action mode. HDR is converted automatically, but off is best.

    4Record

    • One continuous take, three loops as in the diagram, ending each loop where it started.
    • Walk slowly - about 2-3 seconds per step. Turn your body at corners, don't swing the phone.
    • Keep the whole object in frame, filling about half to two-thirds of it.
    • Total length: 1½-4 minutes.

    5Check before you leave

    • Scrub through the video: is every moment sharp, with no jumps, flicker or people walking through?
    • After a run, Splat Studio tells you at which seconds the tracking broke, so you know what to redo.

    Tools

      Setup installs what's missing with Homebrew, rustup and pip, then downloads and builds Brush (15-30 min, once). If Homebrew itself is missing, run ./splat-studio setup in Terminal instead - it needs your password.

      Train again

      Reuses this run's camera poses and trains a new splat with different settings. The existing splat is kept.
